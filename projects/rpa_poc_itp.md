# RPA Proof-of-Concept (PoC) - IT Automations in Public Healthcare

### Scope
- Windows Server Automation: Configuration Checking
- Excel + Chrome Automation: Data Retrieval, Processing & Analysis

### Client
- Government healthcare agency of extra-large scale

### Objectives
- To explore RPA opportunities within a highly secured environment.
- To automate IT routine operations.

### Role
- Analyst Developer, responsible for all technical aspects of the project, including requirements analysis, bot development, and managing change requests.

### Responsibilities
- Optimise business processes by addressing long-standing issues faced by process owners.
- Create best-practice RPA solutions with configurable and exception-handling capabilities within a highly compressed schedule.
- Deploy and test the platform in the client’s highly secure environment.
- Present the progress to process owners through live demonstrations.

### Achievements
- Developed 2 bots in 2 weeks to automate challenging IT back-office operations.
- Received recognition from process owners for efficient prototyping.
- Contributed to securing the project contract for the company.
